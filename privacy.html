Privacy policy: Personal Agent

Personal Agent is used only by its owner. With the owner's permission it accesses:

  • a calendar it creates itself, and the events in it;
  • busy/free times of the owner's calendars, without titles or details.

This data is processed only on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared, or sent to any other service.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
